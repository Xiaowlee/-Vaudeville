### Player response and progression rules
1. **Relevant response:** follow the appropriate authored route. Includes reading cues, semantic equivalents, valid yes/no, uncertainty, relevant improvisation, and awkward but relevant answers where the scene supports them. Relevant refusal is not unrelated speech. Engaging Actor 2 is an authored response, not automatically resistance.
2. **Silence:** reminder and another chance to answer. No off-script increment and no technical increment. Silence alone does not trigger the microphone warning.
3. **Completely unrelated speech:** authored correction, increasing local and overall off-script counters once. Do not classify a recognition/interpretation failure as deliberate unrelated speech.
4. **Technical failure:** recording failure or inability to interpret the attempt. Separate consecutive technical count, no off-script increment. Failures one and two use TECHNICAL RETRY; failure three uses MIC CHECK. Both kinds of technical failure contribute to the same streak. Silence leaves that streak unchanged. Successfully understood speech resets it, including understood unrelated speech. A mic-check acknowledgment alone does not reset it.
5. After MIC CHECK offer retry after checking the device OR continue without speech via that conversation's authored takeover. No off-script penalty or invented consent for this accessibility/recovery path. Taking over resets technical failure state.
6. Local unrelated limit is TWO per conversation/group, including related retry passages. First gets correction/retry; second forces the authored continuation unless the cumulative failure rule fires. A relevant answer after the first allows normal progression. Reset local count when entering a new conversation, not merely each retry passage.
7. Cumulative unrelated count persists across conversations and successful answers. FIFTH overall unrelated response immediately fails, taking priority over local takeover. Intro is exempt from increasing the overall count/failing on that count; local takeover still applies to unrelated intro speech. Intro No/not-ready is a relevant refusal, not a strike.
8. Preserve Act 1's intentional first attempt -> second attempt -> backstage cue rescue sequence. Relevant failed-in-fiction attempts do not increment resistance. Silence asks again rather than silently skipping those attempts.
9. Questions/help/contextual speech that are not genuinely unrelated must not be punished. Where no authored answer exists, treat as clarification/retry without inventing consent. The Twine buttons simulate authored meanings; they do not provide AI recognition or free-speech interpretation.
